Who we are

A small garage with a simple rule: no secrets between us and the buyer.

LEIGHYHOW started as a workshop — not a showroom. We spent years fixing cars, tracing service histories, and chasing down the little details that sellers forget to mention. That's still how we work.

Every car we list goes through the same routine: a full inspection, an honest write-up of its past, and a price record that shows you exactly where the asking price came from. If a car has a story, we tell it — the good and the ugly.

We're not trying to be the biggest dealership in the city. We're trying to be the one you come back to because you trusted what you saw on the page.

How it works

From browsing to handshake — three steps, no surprises.

1

Browse openly

Every car lists its specs, mileage, ownership history and full price record. No login, no "contact us for details."

2

Ask anything

Open the listing, hit Chat about this car, and talk to us directly. We answer questions about condition, service, past repairs — anything.

3

Come see it

If it's still the right car after the chat, we'll set up a viewing at the garage. Bring a friend, a mechanic, whoever you trust.

Why buyers keep coming back

Three things we do that most listings don't.

Transparent pricing

Every car page shows the full price history — every change, every date. You'll know if the number moved, and by how much, before you ever talk to us.

Real ownership stories

We list the actual history we've been able to verify: who owned it, where it lived, what was replaced. If there's a gap in the record, we say so.

Chat, not callback

You message us, we reply in the same thread. No forms, no "a representative will contact you shortly," no phone tag.
